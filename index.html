01 • Executive Overview

Bridging Software Engineering, Health Informatics & Public Revenue Administration

Knoph O. Ayieko is a multidisciplinary Kenyan technologist, entrepreneur and public administrator based in Nairobi. His career reflects a deliberate progression from building resilient software applications and national health data systems to strengthening fiscal compliance and enterprise technology governance.

Guided by the conviction that “efficient systems are the backbone of strong institutions,” Ayieko has contributed across critical national infrastructure—supporting HIV/AIDS and electronic medical record ecosystems (KenyaEMR, CPIMS, KMHFR, NASCOP), deploying educational ICT networks across Kenyan schools and engineering modern full-stack platforms for private-sector enterprises.

As Managing Director of Omni-Legacy Limited and a Graduate Trainee with the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) undergoing specialized training at KESRA, he combines deep technical fluency with disciplined institutional leadership and analytical rigor.

Full-Stack Software Engineering

React, Node.js, Express, Python, Flask, Django, PHP, SQL, Supabase and automated CI/CD workflows.

Tax, Customs & Data Integrity

Corporate tax compliance analysis, customs frameworks and data-driven controls to curb revenue leakage.

Health Informatics & Infrastructure

National health databases (KenyaEMR, CPIMS, KMHFR), GIS mapping, enterprise LANs and cybersecurity governance.
